CardPicker Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026

Overview

CardPicker helps you pick the best credit card from your wallet for each purchase. It is designed to work entirely on your device, with no account and no servers.

Data storage

Your card wallet, reward rules, point values, preferences, and merchant corrections are stored only on your device, in a local on-device database. CardPicker never transmits this data anywhere.

Data collection

CardPicker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically, the app:

  • Requires no account and no sign-in.
  • Includes no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • Never asks for or stores card numbers, CVVs/security codes, passwords, or transaction credentials.
  • Makes no network requests of any kind.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you choose Export in Settings, and are saved to a location you choose. CardPicker never uploads backups anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
