CardPicker Help

How to get the most out of CardPicker

What CardPicker does

CardPicker looks at the reward rules of every card in your wallet and tells you which one earns the most for the purchase you're about to make. It comes with 12 popular cards pre-loaded, and you can add your own or edit any reward rule.

Choose — find the best card

  1. Type the merchant name (e.g. Shell, Chipotle). CardPicker guesses the category automatically.
  2. If the category looks wrong, tap it and pick the right one — CardPicker remembers your correction for that merchant next time.
  3. Cards are ranked by what you'd actually earn. Cash-back cards show a % rate; points cards show their points rate plus an approximate cash value like (≈ 6¢/$) so you can compare across both.
Tip: Rules marked as needing activation (like Discover's and Freedom Flex's quarterly 5%) only count if you've turned them on for that card in the Wallet. Expired promos and used-up caps are filtered out automatically.

Wallet — manage your cards

  • Add cards: tap Add cards to browse the catalog. The 12 built-in seed cards are marked; your own custom cards always appear.
  • Hide seed cards: if you only want to see your own cards in the Add list, go to Settings and turn off Show seed cards in Add list.
  • Edit a card: tap a card, then Edit to change its reward rules, turn bonus activations on/off, set its point currency, or reset it back to the built-in defaults.
  • New custom card: tap + New custom card to add a card that isn't in the catalog. Custom cards can be deleted from the editor; built-in cards can't.

About activations

Some bonuses (for example, the quarterly 5% categories on Discover and Freedom Flex) require you to activate them with the bank first. In the card editor, flip the switch next to the rule once you've activated it — only then will CardPicker count that bonus in Choose.

Settings

  • Export / Import: back up your wallet to a file, or restore from one. Keep the file somewhere safe — it's the only copy outside your phone.
  • Show seed cards in Add list: hides the 12 built-in cards from the Add-cards catalog for a cleaner list.
  • Point values: CardPicker converts points to an approximate cash value so points cards and cash-back cards rank fairly against each other. The defaults are sensible; adjust them in Settings if you value your points differently.

FAQ

The merchant category is wrong. What do I do?

Tap the category and correct it. CardPicker learns that merchant for next time.

Why isn't my 5% quarterly bonus showing up?

Open the card in the Wallet and make sure the activation switch for that rule is on — and that the current date falls inside the bonus window.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything lives in a database on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. See the privacy policy.

I edited a seed card and messed it up. Can I undo that?

Yes — open the card in the editor and use Reset to seed to restore its original reward rules.
